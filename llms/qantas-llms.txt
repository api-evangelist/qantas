# Qantas

> Qantas Airways Limited is Australia's flag carrier and, with its low-cost subsidiary Jetstar, one half of a domestic duopoly alongside Virgin Australia. In the API landscape Qantas is a supplier, not a platform: it publishes no developer portal, no OpenAPI/Swagger/AsyncAPI/GraphQL definition and no public consumer API. Its single API is the Qantas Distribution Platform (QDP) NDC XML API — an IATA New Distribution Capability XML interface for travel agencies, GDSs and aggregators, built with Farelogix (now Accelya) — documented in prose but never as a contract, and gated behind a signed Qantas Distribution Platform Access Agreement plus IATA/ARC/TIDS/VTC accreditation.

Generated by API Evangelist (apievangelist.com) from public Qantas sources on 2026-07-28.
Qantas does not publish an llms.txt; /llms.txt returns 404 on www.qantas.com,
agencyconnect.qantas.com and api.qantas.com.

## What is and is not available

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Protobuf or Postman collection is published by Qantas.
- No NDC schema version, endpoint host, message catalogue or specification is published.
- No SDK, CLI, sandbox, status page or public roadmap exists.
- Access is bilateral: a technology partner signs a Qantas Distribution Platform Access Agreement (subject to Qantas technical and commercial approval) to reach a test environment; an agency holds IATA, ARC, TIDS or VTC accreditation and either nominates a Certified Technology Partner or applies for the QDP portal.
- Do not infer endpoints, operations or schemas for Qantas. There are none published.

## APIs

- [Qantas Distribution Platform NDC XML API](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/ndc): IATA NDC XML interface for shopping, offer and order creation, ticketing, ancillaries and post-booking servicing of Qantas (QF) content. No public endpoint.

## Policies and contract terms

- [NDC Shopping Policy](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/policies/agent-information/ndc-shopping-policy): fair use — maximum acceptable Look-to-Book ratio 2500:1, with offer limitation, request throttling and access restriction as remedies.
- [Distribution Surcharge Policy](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/policies/fares-and-ticketing/distribution-surcharge-policy): per-segment YR fee by booking channel.
- [NDC Booking Time Limits](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/policies/fares-and-ticketing/ndc-booking-time-limits): Ticketing Time Limit (TTL) and Price Guarantee Ticketing Time Limit (PGTTL) semantics.
- [Standard Agency Terms and Conditions](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/standard-agency-terms-conditions): effective 1 July 2025; 30 days' notice of amendment; accreditation requirements; ticketing authority at Qantas' sole discretion.
- [QDP portal terms and conditions](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/ndc/distribution-platform-portal/terms-conditions)
- [Privacy and security](https://www.qantas.com/en-au/help/policies/privacy-and-security): Qantas Privacy Policy, updated 1 May 2026.

## Access

- [Premium NDC](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/ndc/premium): widest content, zero surcharge; invitation only or Expression of Interest behind login.
- [Certified Technology Partners](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/ndc/approved-technology-partners): 13 partners — Aaron Group, Aeronology, AirGateway, Amadeus, Clarity TTS, Duffel, Mystifly, Sabre, TPConnects, Travelfusion, Travelport, TravelSky, Verteil.
- [Register for the QDP portal](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/ndc/distribution-platform-portal/register): IATA/TIDS/ARC prerequisite; review up to 7 business days.
- [Technology Partner Support Helpdesk](https://agencyconnect.qantas.com/en-au/policies/agent-information/technology-partner-support-helpdesk): per-partner support contacts.
